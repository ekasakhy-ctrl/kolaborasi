Dama Aji Kurniawan (15)

Nowadays, creative recycling has become one of the best ways to protect the environment. Many computer cases are abandoned because they are old or damaged. Instead of letting them become useless waste, people can turn them into new products. One interesting innovation is making a bookshelf from an old computer case. This idea gives electronic waste a second life.

The process starts by selecting a suitable computer case. The case is then opened, and its internal parts are removed to create enough space. After that, the metal surface is cleaned to remove dust and dirt. Some wooden materials are also prepared to support the books and create separate storage levels.

Once the materials are ready, the wooden shelves are placed inside the computer case. They are adjusted to fit the size of the case and attached securely. Then, the outside is decorated with interesting designs to make it more appealing. Additional decorations can also be added to give the bookshelf a modern and technological appearance.

The finished product is a creative bookshelf made from recycled computer equipment. It provides space for storing books and other learning materials. Moreover, its unique design can become an attractive decoration for classrooms, offices, or bedrooms. By reusing an old computer case, people can reduce waste,save materials, and develop their creativity. This proves that unwanted electronic equipment can still have a useful purpose.

Electronic waste is one of the environmental problems caused by the development of technology. Old computer cases are often thrown away when they are no longer used. However, computer cases can still be reused to make useful products. One creative idea is transforming an old computer case into a bookshelf. This product helps reduce electronic waste and supports the 3R principle.

The process begins by preparing an unused computer case. First, the computer case is cleaned to remove dust and dirt. Then, the unnecessary parts inside the case are removed. After that, the inside of the case is measured to determine the size of the shelves.

Next, wooden boards are prepared and cut according to the required size. The boards are then installed inside the computer case to create bookshelves. After the shelves are firmly attached, the case is arranged and decorated to make it look more attractive. Finally, books and other stationery can be placed inside the bookshelf.

As a result, an old computer case is transformed into a useful and creative bookshelf. This product can be used to store books and stationery while also decorating a room. Making this bookshelf helps reduce electronic waste and gives unused materials a new purpose. Therefore, this project is a simple example of applying the 3R principle: Reduce, Reuse, and recycle

Recycling is an important activity to reduce environmental pollution. Many old computer cases are no longer used and are often thrown away. In fact, they can be turned into something useful and creative. One example is transforming an old computer case into a bookshelf. This product combines creativity, technology, and environmental awareness.

First, an old computer case is collected and cleaned carefully. All unnecessary electronic components are removed from the inside. The size of the case is measured to determine the appropriate shelf positions. After that, wooden boards are prepared to be used as the bookshelf levels.

Next, the wooden boards are installed inside the computer case to create several storage spaces. The shelves are arranged according to their size and function. The outside of the case is then decorated with stickers, labels, or creative designs. Finally, the bookshelf is checked to make sure that it is strong and stable enough to hold books.

Finally, the old computer case becomes a unique and useful bookshelf. It can be used to organize books, notebooks, and stationery. Besides being functional, it also makes a room look more creative and attractive. This innovation helps reduce electronic waste and teaches people to reuse old materials instead of throwing them away.
